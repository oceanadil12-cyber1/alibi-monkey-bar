Antonio Edwards

Performing at the Alibi since 2015

Known for his powerful voice, versatility, and engaging stage presence, Antonio performs an exciting mix of American Standards, Motown, Disco, ’80s favorites, Pop, R&B, and those big, booming diva power ballads. Also performs alongside LeNora Jaye in their popular “DUETS” show.

Brian Mason

The ultimate weekend soundtrack

Brian brings the vocal chops while providing the ultimate soundtrack to kick off your weekend. From pop and R&B hits through the decades to diva ballads, Brian serves a touch of soulfulness with a dash of crooner vibes.

Pop R&B Soul Diva Ballads

LeNora Jaye

NYC native, South Florida soul

Grew up surrounded by Gospel, Jazz, Bebop, Motown, Opera, and Cabaret. LeNora brings powerful vocals, soulful style, and vibrant energy. Blending Soul, Jazz, Motown, Disco, and classic R&B, her performances celebrate timeless favorites with a touch of glamour and plenty of GROOVE.

Soul Jazz Motown Disco R&B

Franklin Llovera

Latin flavor, powerhouse vocals

Franklin brings powerful vocals, Latin flavor, and warm charismatic energy to the stage. His performances blend pop, soul, Latin favorites, and audience requests, with a natural connection and spontaneity that make every show feel personal.

Latin Pop Soul Audience Requests

Miss Bouvèé

Theatrical entertainer & LGBTQIA+ activist

Miss Bouvèé is a live-singing female impersonator and theatrical entertainer known for show-stopping vocals, high camp, and staunch activism in the LGBTQIA+ community.

Natasha Ricketts

The Purple Goddess

The Purple Goddess brings powerhouse vocals, quick wit, and plenty of variety to any stage. Blending pop, rock, Broadway, and throwbacks, her performances are packed with big laughs and shameless flirting — because everyone is welcome in her world.

Pop Rock Broadway Throwbacks

Yara Rey

Cuban roots, diva energy

A versatile singer who celebrates her Cuban roots through Latin music, while embracing iconic diva hits, Broadway classics, and much more. Her powerful voice and vibrant stage presence create an unforgettable experience for every audience.

Jennifer McClain

Alibi staple since 2008

Jennifer has been entertaining Wilton Manors audiences for over two decades and a staple at Alibi since 2008. With a vast repertoire at her fingertips, she can easily slip from a sultry torch song to a high-energy pop anthem and then into a comedic Broadway number.
