## The basics
- Name: Georgie's Alibi Monkey Bar ("The Alibi"), a restaurant, bar and live-entertainment venue.
- Address: 2266 Wilton Drive, Wilton Manors, FL 33305 (the heart of Wilton Manors, South Florida).
- Phone: (954) 565-2526
- Email: info@alibimonkeybar.com
- Instagram: @alibimonkeybar (lineup changes and special events are posted there).
- Open every day, Monday to Sunday, 11:00 AM to 2:00 AM.
- Parking: street parking and public lots nearby.
- Open since 1997 (29 years). An LGBTQ+ community cornerstone; everyone is welcome.
- No cover charge for the weekly shows (every night).

## Spaces
- Main Bar: large, social; home of the Main Stage (DJs, dance parties, karaoke, live music).
- Monkey Bar: intimate room with its own stage for up-close drag, comedy and live music.
- Outdoor Patio: open-air dining with a tropical feel.

## Food and ordering
- Scratch kitchen: burgers, wings, sandwiches, salads, flatbreads, Mexican and Asian dishes, desserts.
- Online ordering for pickup or delivery is on the Menu page (/menu.html), paid by card.
- Pickup: curbside by the front door (look for the sign, call when you arrive) or at the host stand on the patio. Have your order number and name ready.
- Food allergies or dietary needs: tell your server before ordering; for special requests in advance, call the bar.
- Drinks are ordered at the bar and are not sold online.

## Happy hour and drink deals
- Happy Hour every day 4:00 PM to 6:45 PM (food specials are on the Menu page).
- 2-for-1 drinks until 8 PM, plus nightly drink specials that go with the shows (see the lineup).

## Reservations
- Walk-ins are always welcome; a reservation guarantees a table.
- Book with the reservation form on the Contact page (/contact.html#reserve) or call (954) 565-2526.
- Reservation requests are confirmed by the team by phone or email, not instantly.
- Weekend reservations: book about 48 hours ahead.
- Parties of 8 or more: please call directly.
- Areas you can ask for: Main Bar, Monkey Bar or Outdoor Patio.
- Tuesday's The Groove Session is "dinner and a show": reservations recommended.

## Private events (/private-events.html)
- Birthdays, corporate events, anniversaries, engagements, graduations, showers and more.
- Add-ons: drag performers, DJs, live music, custom catering (passed appetizers to plated dinners), open bar / hosted bar / cash bar, custom cocktail menus, bottle service, decorations.
- Spaces: Monkey Bar (30-60 guests), Outdoor Patio (40-80 guests), full venue buyout (up to 300 guests).
- Monkey Bar Mondays: the Monkey Bar is closed to the public on Mondays and can be booked privately.
- Packages (starting prices; the events team quotes the final price):
  - Bronze from $500: reserved seating area, appetizer platter, cash bar, up to 20 guests, dedicated server.
  - Gold from $1,500 (most popular): private Monkey Bar area, custom food menu, 2-hour open bar, up to 60 guests, DJ or live entertainment, custom decorations.
  - Platinum from $5,000: full venue buyout, custom plated menu, 4-hour premium bar, up to 300 guests, full entertainment lineup, event coordinator.
- To plan one: the contact form (/contact.html, subject "Private Event") or call (954) 565-2526.

## Entertainment
- Shows seven nights a week: live music, drag, karaoke, Latin nights, DJs, go-go dancers.
- Go-go dancers (Anthony, Ever, Isaiah, James, Jose, Luis, Roberto) on Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights. Please tip the dancers. (/dancers.html)
- Wednesday "Strip for a Tip" amateur night on the Main Stage: $200 cash top prize, free to enter.
- Monday "5 Minutes of Fame" karaoke: every time you sing, you get a shot.
- Performers, DJs and entertainers who want to book a gig: use the contact form (subject "Entertainment / Booking").
