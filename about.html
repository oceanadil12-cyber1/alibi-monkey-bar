The Story of The Alibi

For 29 years, Georgie's Alibi Monkey Bar has been the heart and soul of Wilton Manors. What started as a neighborhood bar has grown into South Florida's premier destination for dining, drinks, and world-class entertainment.

Our award-winning venue combines exceptional food from our scratch kitchen, craft cocktails from our expert mixologists, and unforgettable entertainment featuring the region's top performers.

We've built more than a bar — we've built a community. A welcoming space where everyone is celebrated, every night is memorable, and every visit feels like coming home.

Our Mission

At The Alibi, we believe in creating spaces where everyone belongs. We're proud to be a cornerstone of the Wilton Manors community — a place where diversity is celebrated, creativity thrives, and every guest is treated like family.

We're committed to delivering exceptional experiences through quality food, inventive drinks, boundary-pushing entertainment, and genuine hospitality. Every team member shares our passion for making your visit special.
